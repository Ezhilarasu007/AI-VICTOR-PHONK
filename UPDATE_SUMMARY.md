## 🎨 Theme Usage
To change themes:
1. Go to Settings
2. Under "App Preferences"
3. Click on theme buttons: Default, Red, Black, Blue, Pink
4. Theme is saved automatically

## 🖼️ Wallpaper Usage
To add custom wallpaper:
1. Go to Settings
2. Under "App Preferences"
3. Click "Choose File" next to "Custom Wallpaper"
4. Select your image
5. Wallpaper is applied and saved

To clear wallpaper:
1. Go to Settings
2. Under "App Preferences"
3. Click "Clear Wallpaper"
