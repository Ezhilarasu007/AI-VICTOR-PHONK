Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This build has no account system or application backend. It does not send your prompts, generated audio, library, or other app data to Firebase or an AI provider. App state is held in memory in your browser and is cleared when the page is closed.

2. How We Use Your Information

This build does not run AI audio generation or process payments. Google Analytics 4 is configured and may collect website usage and device data according to Google's terms and the property's settings. Your browser may also send standard connection data to the hosting provider.

3. Data Sharing

Website analytics data is processed by Google Analytics. No prompts or audio are sent to an AI provider by this build. Third-party hosting and browser services are governed by their own privacy terms.

4. Data Security

Because there is no account or backend, do not enter sensitive personal information into this demo.

5. Your Rights

Since this build does not retain app data on a server, closing the page clears its in-memory app state.

6. Contact

For questions about this project, use the contact options on the project repository.
